# Ethics, Governance, and KUHS Submission Pathway

## Ethical classification

The planned study uses public, non-identifiable information about approved drugs. It does not enrol people, access patient records, collect biological specimens, intervene in treatment, or use animals.

That makes informed consent and participant procedures inapplicable. It does **not** authorise the investigators to self-declare ethics exemption. A written determination from the appropriate Institutional Ethics Committee must be obtained before the technical pilot or analysis.

The request should plainly state:

- public secondary-data study;
- drug, not person, is the unit of analysis;
- no identifiable or individual-level data;
- no recruitment, intervention, specimen, or animal;
- public FDA, PubChem, and open-model sources;
- exact study title and current protocol version;
- request for the committee's formal decision on review/exemption status.

## KUHS rules confirmed from the official guideline indexed in August 2026

The official KUHS Research Appreciation Award guideline currently located on the KUHS site states that:

- the guide can forward only one student application per year;
- the guide should be regular full-time faculty with a KUHS FEP ID in the student's institution;
- the guide cannot be changed during the study;
- the same project must not be submitted elsewhere for another award/scholarship/program;
- the Application Attestation Form and IEC approval letter are mandatory attachments;
- the protocol limits are title 25 words, introduction 300, objectives 100, methodology 800, implications 100, and references 300 words in Vancouver style;
- the institutional plagiarism certificate must be below 10%;
- the ethics letter must name the student, guide, exact title, and approval status, and another project’s letter is not accepted.

These rules come from a guideline file under a 2024 KUHS path. The live award notification and portal instructions can change and override it. Recheck them immediately before submission.

## Anonymity boundary

The source drafts state that identifying information should not appear in the scientific proposal. The official guideline's report section explicitly rejects reports that contain student/guide names, contact details, or college name and places identifying data in online/attestation fields.

Because the indexed text is clearer for the final report than for every proposal field, use the conservative workflow:

1. Keep the scientific protocol free of names, phone numbers, email addresses, and institution name.
2. Put identifying information only in the portal and mandatory attestation/ethics documents.
3. Confirm the proposal-specific anonymity rule against the current call or portal before upload.
4. Scan document properties, comments, tracked changes, headers, footers, filenames, and embedded metadata—not only visible text.

## KUHS protocol package

Maintain two separate artefacts:

### Master scientific protocol

The detailed repository document that defines all methods, limitations, and reproducibility requirements. It is not constrained to the short portal word limits.

### KUHS submission protocol

An anonymised, word-limited version containing exactly the required sections:

1. Title: up to 25 words.
2. Introduction: up to 300 words.
3. Objectives: up to 100 words.
4. Methodology: up to 800 words.
5. Implications: up to 100 words.
6. References: up to 300 words, Vancouver style.

Do not combine required sections. Word counts should be calculated automatically and manually checked after conversion to the upload format.

## Required pre-submission confirmations

- [ ] Student is eligible under the current call and has not exceeded application limits.
- [ ] Guide is current regular full-time faculty with a valid KUHS FEP ID.
- [ ] Guide is in the student's institution and is forwarding no other student that year.
- [ ] Exact title is identical across protocol, IEC letter, and portal.
- [ ] Written IEC determination is specific to this project and names student/guide/title/status as required.
- [ ] Application Attestation Form is the current version, completed, signed, stamped, and within file-size limits.
- [ ] Scientific protocol is anonymised according to the live portal rule.
- [ ] All six protocol sections are separate and within word limits.
- [ ] References have been checked against primary bibliographic records.
- [ ] Institutional plagiarism certificate is below 10%.
- [ ] Conflicts, funding, and use of public/model resources are declared.
- [ ] PDF opens correctly, contains no comments or tracked changes, and meets size limits.
- [ ] Deadline and final-submit action are confirmed; a local copy and submission receipt are retained.

## Research integrity

This repository can organise and support the work, but the student and guide remain responsible for the scientific content. All text, code, data transformations, exclusions, and results must be reviewed and understood by them.

Required conduct:

- cite ideas and reused methods;
- never fabricate a citation, result, eligibility decision, or ethics status;
- preserve raw data and negative/failed outputs;
- report deviations rather than rewriting the plan as if they were pre-specified;
- disclose AI assistance according to institutional and publication policy;
- do not paste identifiable institutional documents into public artefacts;
- publish only data and model-derived material permitted by source licences.

## Data protection

There is no personal research data, but operational documents may contain names, signatures, contact details, or identifiers. Keep attestation forms, ethics letters, and portal receipts outside the public repository in an access-restricted institutional folder. The public reproducibility archive should contain only non-identifiable scientific artefacts.

## Governance for amendments

After protocol lock:

1. Describe the problem.
2. State whether outcomes were viewed.
3. List the proposed change and alternatives.
4. Assess effect on the estimand, bias, schedule, and KUHS/IEC documents.
5. Obtain guide and relevant expert approval.
6. Seek IEC notification/approval if required.
7. Version the amendment before running the changed analysis.
8. Report both the original plan and deviation in the final document.
